A diffuser spreads a hair dryer's airflow over a wider area. For naturally wavy hair, that can help dry the hair while disturbing the wave pattern less than a direct nozzle. It will not create the same result on every texture, but a consistent routine makes it easier to see what your hair responds to.

Start with clean, damp hair. Have a diffuser attachment, a microfiber towel or cotton T-shirt, and one lightweight styling product ready. A mousse often suits fine hair; a light gel can offer more hold. Follow the product directions and add heat protectant if your dryer or product instructions call for it.

## Set the waves before drying

1. **Remove excess water gently.** Squeeze the ends upward with the towel. Avoid rubbing the hair back and forth, which separates the strands before they can form clumps.
2. **Apply one styling product.** Spread a modest amount between your palms, then smooth it over the lengths and scrunch upward from the ends. Keep heavy creams away from fine roots.
3. **Choose your part.** Once drying begins, moving the part repeatedly can break the shape you just set.

## Use the diffuser in two stages

**First, hover at the roots.** Set the dryer to low airflow and low or medium heat. Hold the diffuser a short distance from the scalp and move around the head without pushing the hair around. This starts drying the roots while the lengths keep their shape.

**Then, cup the ends.** Place a section of ends into the diffuser bowl. Lift it toward the scalp and hold it still for several seconds before lowering it and moving to the next section. Switch the dryer off while moving between sections if the airflow scatters your waves.

Stop when the hair is mostly dry and let the rest air-dry. Repeatedly checking or scrunching damp sections can create frizz.

## Finish without flattening

If a gel leaves a firm cast, wait until the hair is fully dry before scrunching it gently with clean hands. Lift the roots with your fingertips for volume, but avoid brushing through the finished waves unless you want a softer, less defined result.

## Troubleshooting

- **Waves look stringy:** Try less product next time, or apply it to wetter hair so it distributes more evenly.
- **The crown is flat:** Dry the roots first while tilting your head from side to side. Avoid piling all the lengths on top of the scalp.
- **Frizz appears during drying:** Reduce airflow and keep each section still in the diffuser bowl. Handling hair while it is half-dry often makes the pattern less defined.

Change one part of the routine at a time. That makes it easier to tell whether product amount, airflow or drying time made the difference.
