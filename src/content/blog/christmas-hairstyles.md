December asks more of your hair than any other month: office parties, family photographs, Christmas Day lunch and a carol service can all land in the same fortnight, and every one comes with a camera. The good news is that festive hair rarely needs a brand-new skill. Most looks below start with a style your hair already does well — a bun, a wave, a plait — and add one deliberate detail: a bow, a pearl pin, a sleeker finish. It is the same approach we recommend for [homecoming hairstyles](/hairstyles/occasion-hairstyles/homecoming-hairstyles/): upgrade one thing, not everything. If you are browsing holiday hairstyles with different events in mind, use the sections by plan — updos for formal evenings, waves for photographs, braids for long days, five-minute finishes for surprises.

## Elegant Party Updos

Updos are the formal end of the list: hair off the shoulders, nothing to rearrange between courses, and a clear view of earrings and necklines. All four work best on day-old hair, which grips pins better than freshly washed lengths.

### 1. Low Chignon with a Pearl Pin

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-01.webp" alt="Low chignon bun with a pearl pin on brunette hair" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

Twist the hair into a low bun at the nape, pin it, then slide a pearl pin through one side. The chignon works on medium and long hair, straight or wavy, and slight looseness looks intentional rather than messy. It suits anyone who wants polish without height — the natural choice for Christmas Day lunch or a formal dinner.

### 2. Sleek High Bun with a Velvet Bow

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-02.webp" alt="Sleek high bun finished with a black velvet bow, seen from behind" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

Brush the hair into a high ponytail, twist it into a smooth bun and finish with a velvet bow at the base. Smoothing the front with a brush and a little gel separates it from a gym bun; the bow does the festive work. It suits straighter hair, high necklines and statement earrings — an office-party look that will not move all night.

### 3. Modern French Twist

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-03.webp" alt="Modern French twist updo on auburn hair in profile" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

The updated French twist is softer than the classic: gather the hair at the nape, twist it upwards into a vertical roll and pin along the seam, keeping a little volume at the front. It needs medium to long hair and one practice run, but nothing else here looks as expensive. Best for formal dressing — a carol service or a Christmas Eve dinner.

### 4. Braided Crown Updo

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-04.webp" alt="Braided crown updo on honey blonde hair with loose tendrils" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

Plait two braids from above each ear, wrap them over the top of the head and pin the ends underneath, with the back gathered into a low bun. The braiding does the decorating, so no accessories are needed. It suits medium and long hair, and it is made for family photographs — detailed in front, tidy behind, intact after a day of guests.

## Glam Waves and Curls

If your event is mostly about photographs, wear your hair down. Waves catch the light, frame the face and make even a simple outfit look finished. These four run from red-carpet polish to natural texture.

### 5. Hollywood Glamour Waves

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-05.webp" alt="Glossy Hollywood glamour waves with a deep side part on dark hair" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

The full glamour option: curls made in one direction, cooled in clips and brushed into a single glossy S-wave with a deep side part. It is the most time-consuming look here — allow the best part of an hour — and the most striking in photographs. Our step-by-step [Hollywood glamour waves](/hairstyles/waves-curls/hollywood-glamour-waves/) guide covers the full sequence. Save it for the biggest party of your season.

### 6. Soft Brushed-Out Curls with a Deep Side Part

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-06.webp" alt="Soft brushed-out curls swept over a deep side part on chestnut hair" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

The relaxed relative of Hollywood waves: curl in large sections, brush everything out, then sweep it over a deep side part so the curls fall in one soft curtain. It takes about fifteen minutes and works on medium and long hair. The family-photo favourite — flattering from every angle and soft enough for daytime.

### 7. Bouncy Blowout Curls

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-07.webp" alt="Bouncy blowout curls with root volume on honey blonde hair" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

A blow-dry look rather than a curled one: big, springy curls with volume at the roots, made with a round brush or large rollers and left glossy rather than sprayed stiff. It suits medium and long hair that holds a blowout well — polished for Christmas morning photographs, still comfortable by the evening film. Refresh on day two with dry shampoo at the roots.

### 8. Defined Natural Curls with a Sparkly Clip

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-08.webp" alt="Defined natural curls pinned back on one side with a sparkly crystal clip" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

If your hair is naturally curly, the festive version is definition plus one jewel: refresh and define your curls as usual, then pin one side back with a crystal or pearl clip. The clip opens the face for photographs and stops one side falling forwards. It suits every curl pattern and any event: ten minutes, no heat, nothing that fights your texture.

## Braids and Half-Up Styles

The middle ground: hair partly up, partly down, with detail that survives a long day. Braids and half-up styles are also the most forgiving option on hair that will not hold a curl.

### 9. Half-Up Waves with a Satin Ribbon

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-09.webp" alt="Half-up half-down waves tied with a cream satin ribbon, seen from behind" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

Take the top section back and tie it with a length of satin ribbon over the elastic, leaving soft waves underneath. The ribbon colour is the whole styling decision — black for evening, cream or deep red for daytime. It suits medium and long hair and nearly every plan, from office party to Christmas morning. Our [wedding guest half-up waves](/hairstyles/occasion-hairstyles/wedding-guest-half-up-waves/) guide shows the polished, pinned version.

### 10. Halo Crown Braid with Loose Waves

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-10.webp" alt="Halo crown braid with loose waves below it on light brown hair" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

Plait a single braid around the hairline like a halo — or cheat with two plaits pinned over the top — and leave the lengths below in loose waves. It looks intricate and photographs beautifully, but the real advantage is practical: the front is fully secured, so nothing falls into your face. It suits medium and long hair, and it is the strongest choice here for Christmas Day.

### 11. Loose Fishtail Side Braid

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-11.webp" alt="Loose fishtail braid draped over one shoulder on long dark blonde hair" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

Sweep all the hair over one shoulder and fishtail-plait it loosely, pulling the sections gently apart for a soft, full finish. A fishtail looks far more complicated than it is, and worn to the side it stays visible in photographs instead of hidden down your back. It suits long hair best, and it is ideal for cosy gatherings and long lunches.

## Quick Holiday Hairstyles: Five-Minute Festive Finishes

For the plans you hear about that morning, or the party that starts an hour after work: three finishes that make an ordinary style unmistakably festive, each in about five minutes.

### 12. Low Ponytail with a Velvet Bow

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-12.webp" alt="Sleek low ponytail tied with a large black velvet bow, seen from behind" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

Smooth the hair into a low ponytail, wrap a small section around the elastic, and clip a velvet bow above it. On straight hair it looks minimal and expensive; on wavy hair, soft and deliberate. It is the fastest route from desk to office party on this list, and the bow comes straight back out the next morning.

### 13. Hair Tinsel Woven Through Loose Waves

<figure class="article-image"><img src="/images/articles/christmas-hairstyles/christmas-hairstyles-13.webp" alt="Loose dark waves with gold hair tinsel strands catching the light" width="896" height="1200" loading="lazy" decoding="async" /></figure>

A few strands of fine tinsel tied or clipped in at the roots catch the light every time you move, turning plain loose waves into a party style with no restyling at all. Gold suits warm-toned hair; silver shows best on dark and cool-toned hair. Our [hair tinsel](/hairstyles/occasion-hairstyles/hair-tinsel/) guide covers putting it in and taking it out cleanly. Wear it for the big night out.

### 14. Pearl Clips on a Sleek Side Sweep

Smooth the hair flat on one side of a deep parting and stack two or three pearl or crystal clips along the tucked section. It works on any length from a bob upwards, on straight hair or smoothed waves, in less time than finding your keys. The answer for short notice and short hair alike — a Christmas lunch or an unplanned photograph.

## Taking the Same Looks into New Year's Eve

New Year's Eve wants the same shapes with the contrast turned up. Sleeken the finishes: the low ponytail becomes glass-smooth, the high bun loses its softness, the side sweep gets lacquered flat. Swap velvet for metal and crystal — a silver bow, a rhinestone clip, silver tinsel rather than gold. Waves go glossier rather than bigger: brush them out once more and add shine spray. For the fully formal version of any of these, our [prom hairstyles](/hairstyles/occasion-hairstyles/prom-hairstyles/) guide applies the same logic at maximum polish. And pin everything you would normally leave to spray — the night runs later.

## How to Make Your Christmas Hairstyle Last All Evening

Five habits decide whether a party style is still in place at midnight:

- **Start on day-old hair.** Freshly washed hair is slippery; hair washed the night before grips pins, holds curl and keeps ponytails tight.
- **Curl tighter than the finish you want.** Curls relax noticeably over the first two hours; what looks too bouncy at 6pm is perfect by 8pm.
- **Pin structure, spray last.** Pins and elastics hold a style; hairspray only protects it. Pin anything that must not move, then spray in light layers.
- **Let heat styles cool completely.** A wave or curl is set as it cools, so brush out or dress the hair only once it is cool to the touch.
- **Pack a two-minute rescue kit.** Four bobby pins, a mini hairspray and one spare elastic turn a slipping section into a non-event.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best hairstyle for a Christmas party?

The one that matches how formal the party is and how long it runs. For a big evening party, Hollywood waves or the braided crown updo photograph best and hold for hours. For an office party, the sleek high bun or the ribbon half-up style read festive without looking as though you tried too hard. If in doubt, choose the low chignon.

### Should I wear my hair up or down at Christmas?

Both work; decide by event, not by rule. Wear it down for photographs and shorter events, where movement and shine matter most. Wear it up for long days — cooking, travelling between houses, dancing — where loose hair gets in the way and loses its finish. Half-up styles are the genuine compromise when the plan involves both.

### Which Christmas hairstyle photographs best?

Styles with a clear shape and a smooth surface: Hollywood waves, the deep side-part curls and the braided crown updo all read clearly on camera. Very loose, wispy finishes can disappear in flash photography, and heavy matte texture spray can look dull. Whatever you choose, take one test photograph with flash before you leave.

### What can I do in five minutes before an office Christmas party?

Use one of the finishes from the quick section: a velvet bow on a low ponytail, pearl clips on a side sweep, or tinsel through the waves you already have. All three work on hair styled for an ordinary workday, need no heat, and come out again the next morning. Keep the bow or clips in your desk drawer and you are ready for every plan.

### What Christmas hairstyles work on short hair?

Bobs and shorter cuts should lean on finish and accessories rather than updos. A sleek deep parting with pearl clips, a waved bob with one side tucked and pinned, or a smooth, glossy blowout all read as occasion hair. The bun-based looks need more length, but the clip, bow and tinsel finishes all scale down.

### How far in advance should I practise my Christmas hairstyle?

Once, properly, before the day — ideally the weekend before, wearing the style for a few hours. A trial tells you how long it really takes (most looks here need 20 to 45 minutes, Hollywood waves nearer an hour) and whether it survives movement. Practise the updos and the halo braid in particular: both are simple once your hands know the route.

## Final Thoughts

Christmas hair does not need a new skill for every event — it needs a small repertoire: one updo you can pin without thinking, one down style that photographs well, and one five-minute finish for surprises. Learn those three and every plan in December is covered, from the carol service to the last photograph.
